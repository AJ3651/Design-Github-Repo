Ecology & Society

Through computational art, this section repositions the relationship between ecology and society. Water, wind, insects, sensors, maps, platforms, and algorithms are treated not simply as materials or tools, but as relational agents that reveal how power, memory, and perception circulate through contemporary life. Rather than offering fixed answers, these practices use specific ecological contexts to question our dependence on precision, connection, and intelligence, and to imagine new ways of understanding environment, technology, and society.
